# Phase VII-B final empirical gate decision

## Decision: GO FOR MANUSCRIPT RECONSTRUCTION

The approved six-year panel has 216 complete state-year records. It preserves
the verified source lineage for IGR, state-government net FAAC, domestic debt,
external debt, valuation conventions, and source-date qualifications. All 216
combined-exposure identities pass. The 180 exact Shapley reconstructions pass
the registered absolute tolerance of 1e-10. An independently implemented Julia
calculation agrees with the governing R results, with a maximum observed
difference below 7e-15.

## Conditions that remain binding

- Describe the denominator as IGR plus annual state-government net FAAC, not
  total state revenue.
- Keep domestic and external debt separate before constructing combined exposure.
- Identify the 2021 NGN412.99 rate as inferred from matched official totals.
- Retain DMO provisional labels and the 25 domestic non-year-end state-year
  observations in every relevant table note and sensitivity discussion.
- Interpret Shapley values as accounting allocations, never causal drivers.
- Do not infer fiscal capacity, fiscal performance, debt sustainability, or
  household outcomes from these indicators.

## Authorization boundary

This decision authorizes evidence-locked manuscript reconstruction and the
associated editorial review. It does not authorize new household models,
state-capacity indices, or any change to the source panel without a separately
versioned protocol amendment.
