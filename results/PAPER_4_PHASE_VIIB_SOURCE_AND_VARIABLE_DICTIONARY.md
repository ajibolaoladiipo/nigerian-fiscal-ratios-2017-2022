# Phase VII-B source and variable dictionary

`igr_ngn`: verified annual NBS internally generated revenue.
`faac_ngn`: verified January--December state-government net-FAAC disbursements.
`two_component_revenue_ngn`: IGR plus state-government net FAAC only.
`domestic_debt_ngn`: official DMO domestic debt stock; `external_debt_usd`: official DMO external stock before valuation.
All ratios are descriptive stock-to-two-component-annual-revenue quantities.
