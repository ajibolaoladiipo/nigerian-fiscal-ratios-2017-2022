# Public Budgeting & Finance editorial readiness assessment

## Strengths

The completed panel supports a focused public-finance measurement contribution:
six annual observations, separately retained domestic and external debt stocks,
historically documented valuation conventions, source-date flags, and a
verified two-component revenue denominator. The R and Julia implementations
independently reproduce the exact accounting decomposition.

## Editorial risk

The article must not present accounting-ratio differences as a discovery in
themselves. Its value lies in the empirical consequences: component-specific
rank persistence, valuation-sensitive comparison, offsetting accounting
contributions, and visible reporting-date restrictions. The paper must give
equal prominence to the provisional DMO status, the 2021 inferred valuation,
and the fact that `IGR + net FAAC` is not total state revenue.

## Assessment

The evidence is adequate for a carefully scoped *Public Budgeting & Finance*
submission after a manuscript-reconstruction and citation audit. It is not
adequate for claims about fiscal performance, debt sustainability, state
capacity, or household outcomes. A full replication archive must contain code,
the derived approved panel, source manifests, and lawful source links; original
documents should be redistributed only where their rights permit.
