# Phase VII-B six-year contribution assessment

## What the reconstructed evidence establishes

The six-year panel supplies a result that the prior three-snapshot design could
not: component-specific state comparisons can be followed annually, under a
fixed source protocol. External-debt-exposure ranks are consistently more
persistent than domestic-debt-exposure ranks. Across the five consecutive
comparisons, external Spearman correlations range from 0.943 to 0.986; domestic
correlations range from 0.829 to 0.868; and combined-exposure correlations range
from 0.824 to 0.911. Stable rank order is descriptive evidence only. It does
not validate any ratio as fiscal capacity.

The annual accounting results also show why combined comparisons cannot be read
as simple debt changes. The mean combined-exposure change was -0.382 in
2017--18, +0.071 in 2018--19, +0.188 in 2019--20, -0.087 in 2020--21, and
+0.006 in 2021--22. In each transition, domestic debt, dollar debt, valuation,
and the two-component revenue denominator can offset one another. These are
exact mathematical allocations, not causal explanations of borrowing or revenue
behaviour.

## Measurement consequence

Revaluing all observations at the 2022 NGN448.08/US$ convention changes the
combined-exposure rank of 23 states in 2017, 21 in 2018, 20 in 2019, and 12 in
2020; the maximum displacement is five ranks. This is not an alternative
official debt series. It demonstrates that historical valuation conventions are
part of the definition of the reported comparison. The bounded 2021
412.985--412.995 rounding check changes no rank, so the source rounding of the
2021 implied convention is not material at the registered precision.

## Contribution suited to public-finance measurement

The contribution is a source-audited Nigerian case demonstrating how component
separation, historically appropriate foreign-currency valuation, denominator
definition, and source-date metadata affect subnational fiscal comparisons. It
is narrower than a claim about fiscal performance, fiscal sustainability, or
state capacity. Its reusable element is the audit procedure: retain components,
record the valuation convention, state the denominator, and report source-date
qualifications before comparing fiscal ratios across governments.

## Material boundaries

The debt schedules are provisional. Twenty-five of 216 domestic state-year inputs
are not dated 31 December. Restricting to common-date domestic observations
changes both the sample and the descriptive summary; for example, the 2018 mean
combined exposure is 1.535 in the full sample and 1.657 in the 26-state
restriction. The revenue denominator remains a verified two-component aggregate,
not total state revenue. These facts constrain interpretation but do not prevent
a transparent measurement article.
