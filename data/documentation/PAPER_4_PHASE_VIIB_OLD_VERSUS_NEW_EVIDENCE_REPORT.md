# Phase VII-B: historical three-snapshot evidence versus six-year evidence

The 2014, 2017, and 2022 work remains preserved as research history. It is not
overwritten and its denominator-dependent estimates are not treated as governing
evidence for the redesigned article.

The new six-year panel confirms the earlier conceptual boundary: own-source
revenue share measures composition in a stated aggregate, while debt-to-revenue
measures reported stock exposure relative to that aggregate. Neither establishes
general state capacity.

The new evidence changes the empirical basis materially. The redesign excludes
the unresolved 2014 denominator and replaces sparse snapshots with annual
2017--22 component data. The 2022 median combined exposure is 1.597 in the new
source-audited panel; it should not be compared mechanically with the old
three-year value because the analytical denominator and source architecture have
changed. The stronger result is annual: external rankings are persistently more
stable than domestic and combined rankings, while valuation and denominator
components alter measured combined exposure in different years.

No old ratio-dependent conclusion is represented as validated by the new work
unless reproduced from the approved Phase VII-B panel.
