# Release status: local submission-preparation package

Prepared: 10 October 2026

This directory is a local, ready-to-deposit research package. It has no configured public remote and no persistent archive identifier. It must not be described as publicly archived, DOI-citable, or journal-submission-ready until the author:

1. confirms source and derived-data redistribution rights;
2. runs the documented clean-package checks in the deposited environment; the local fresh Julia 1.11.2 check passed on 10 October 2026;
3. deposits the approved package in a public, citable repository;
4. records the permanent URL and DOI in `CITATION.cff` and the manuscript data-availability statement; and
5. commissions or obtains an independent final editorial/referee assessment.

The package intentionally excludes manuscripts and private research records.
