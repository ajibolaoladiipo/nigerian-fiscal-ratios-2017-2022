# Data access and source rights

The analytical panel and derived result files in this release are reproducible data products constructed from publicly issued Nigerian Debt Management Office (DMO) and National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) publications. They contain no survey microdata or confidential material.

Original DMO debt schedules and NBS IGR/FAAC publications are not reproduced in this repository. They should be retrieved from the issuing institutions using the links and source identities in `data/documentation/`. Their copyrights, access terms, and any later revisions remain governed by the original publishers.

Before a public deposit is made, the depositor must confirm that depositing the derived state-year panel and the reproduced figure files is permitted under the relevant source terms. If any restriction is identified, retain the code and source manifest publicly and provide the affected derived input through a lawful access procedure described in the archive record.
